🔥 Bi-Spectral Sub-Pixel Temperature & Flaming Area Inverter (Dozier 1981 / Wooster FRE)

Dual-Channel Mid-IR (3.9µm) / Thermal-IR (11.0µm) Radiance Solver

Physical proof of subpixel detection: Satellite sensors detect fires occupying as little as 0.001% of a pixel footprint because radiance scales with the 4th power of temperature (T⁴). This Dozier inversion solver computes subpixel flaming fraction (p), true combustion temperature (T_f), and biomass burning rate.

ⓘ About FireBridge | NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

FireBridge was developed for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge under the challenge track: "Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots".

It provides a continuous, open-science active fire climatology spanning from the launch of Terra MODIS (2000) to the latest operational VIIRS observations on NOAA-21 (2026), empowering emergency managers, firefighters, land managers, and carbon modelers with consistent long-term fire intelligence.

FireBridge Team (2026). FireBridge: Harmonization of NASA MODIS and VIIRS Active Fire Hotspots (2000–2026). 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge Operational Prototype. Available at: https://github.com/nasa-spaceapps-2026/firebridge

The 2012 Sensor Cliff Paradox

Notice the sudden 3.8x spike in detected fire hotspots in 2012. This was not a sudden explosion in wildfires—it was an artificial sensor cliff caused by the introduction of VIIRS (375m pixels) replacing MODIS (1km pixels).
